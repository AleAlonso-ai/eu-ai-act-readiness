# Timeline

| Date | What applies |
|---|---|
| 1 Aug 2024 | Act enters into force |
| 2 Feb 2025 | Prohibited practices and AI literacy |
| 2 Aug 2025 | General-purpose AI model duties, governance, penalties |
| 2 Aug 2026 | Most remaining rules, including Article 50 transparency |
| 2 Dec 2027 (proposed) | Annex III high-risk systems, previously 2 Aug 2026 |
| 2 Aug 2028 (proposed) | Annex I regulated-product systems, previously 2 Aug 2027 |

## Status of the delay
A provisional political agreement (Digital Omnibus) was reported in 2026 to postpone the high-risk dates. It takes legal effect only when formally adopted and published in the Official Journal. **Confirm the current status before advising anyone.** Last reviewed: October 2026.

## Penalty ceilings
- Prohibited practices: up to EUR 35M or 7% of worldwide turnover
- Most other obligations: up to EUR 15M or 3%
- Incorrect information to authorities: up to EUR 7.5M or 1%
- For SMEs and start-ups, the lower of the two amounts applies
