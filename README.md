# EU AI Act Readiness Toolkit

Practical, plain-language templates for small and mid-size companies preparing for the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Maintained by [TouchOne Governance](https://touchone.ai/governance), with a focus on Finance, Healthcare, tech companies and start-ups.

> **Not legal advice.** This is governance guidance to help you organise your thinking. For legal determinations, work with qualified counsel. Dates and summaries reflect public sources as of October 2026 and must be confirmed against the Official Journal before you rely on them.

## What is in this repo

| [`templates/ai-usage-policy.md`](templates/ai-usage-policy.md) | A starter AI usage policy for staff |
| [`templates/vendor-ai-questionnaire.md`](templates/vendor-ai-questionnaire.md) | Questions to ask AI vendors |
| [`checklists/deployer-checklist.md`](checklists/deployer-checklist.md) | A checklist for companies that use AI systems |

## How to use it

1. Fill in the inventory CSV. Include AI built into tools you already pay for.
2. Work through the Obligations Map for each system.
3. Adopt the usage policy, adapted to your company.
4. Send the vendor questionnaire to your key AI suppliers.
5. Review quarterly, and whenever the law, a system or a vendor changes.

## Keep the dates honest

The high-risk dates were proposed to move under the Digital Omnibus agreement. Check [`docs/timeline.md`](docs/timeline.md) and the Official Journal for what is in force today.
