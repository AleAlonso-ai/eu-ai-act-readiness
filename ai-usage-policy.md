# AI Usage Policy (starter)

*Adapt to your company and have it reviewed by counsel. Version 1.0, owner: ______, review: every 6 months.*

## Purpose
Set clear rules so staff can use AI safely and the company can show how it governs AI.

## Scope
All employees and contractors, and all AI tools, whether company-provided, embedded in software we use, or personal.

## Rules
1. Use only approved AI tools for company work. Request new tools through ______.
2. Never enter confidential data, customer or patient data, credentials or source code into a tool not approved for that data.
3. A person is responsible for every AI output they use. Review before relying on or sending it.
4. Do not use AI for decisions about hiring, credit, insurance, medical care or other significant effects on people without human review and approval from ______.
5. Disclose AI use to customers where required, and label AI-generated content where required.
6. Do not use AI for prohibited purposes, including manipulation, social scoring or emotion recognition of staff.
7. Report AI errors, data exposure and unexpected behaviour to ______ within one business day.

## Governance
- AI owner: ______
- Inventory maintained at: ______
- Training: all staff complete AI literacy training within 30 days of joining and yearly.
- Exceptions require written approval.

## Incidents
Report promptly. We record what happened, which controls failed, who was affected, and what we changed.
