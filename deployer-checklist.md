# Deployer checklist

## Now
- [ ] Inventory every AI system, including vendor-embedded AI
- [ ] Screen for prohibited practices
- [ ] Name an AI governance owner
- [ ] Start AI literacy training for staff who use AI
- [ ] Adopt an AI usage policy

## For each high-risk system
- [ ] Obtain and keep the provider's instructions for use
- [ ] Use the system as instructed
- [ ] Assign trained human oversight with authority to intervene
- [ ] Check that input data you control is relevant and representative
- [ ] Monitor operation; report risks and serious incidents to the provider
- [ ] Keep the logs you control (at least six months)
- [ ] Inform workers and affected people
- [ ] Assess whether a fundamental rights impact assessment is required

## Transparency
- [ ] Tell people when they are interacting with AI
- [ ] Label synthetic content where required

## Keep current
- [ ] Review dates and the Digital Omnibus status quarterly
- [ ] Re-run the Obligations Map when a system or vendor changes
