## 4. Obligations
List only the duties that are yours, by role and tier. Typical items:
- Prohibited-practices screening and AI literacy for staff (apply now)
- Transparency notices for chatbots and synthetic content
- Deployers of high-risk systems: follow instructions for use, assign human oversight, monitor, keep logs you control, inform affected people, report serious incidents, and where required carry out a fundamental rights impact assessment
- Providers of high-risk systems: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity, quality management, conformity assessment, registration, post-market monitoring
